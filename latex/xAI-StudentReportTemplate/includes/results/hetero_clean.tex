
The \textbf{heterogeneous ensemble} achieves strong accuracy (see Table~\ref{tab:hetero_clean}) on the clean dataset.
Oracle accuracy reaches 96.20\%, exceeding the realized ensemble performance.
The moderate oracle gap indicates the presence of complementary expertise among backbones.
However, uniform averaging already captures most of the attainable performance.
Calibration analysis shows that ensemble-level ECE remains non-negligible, indicating that improved accuracy does not automatically imply improved probability calibration.
